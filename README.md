# SQL-project

🎵 Music Store SQL Analysis
    This project showcases SQL-based data analysis performed on a digital music store database. Using PostgreSQL, we explore customer behavior, sales trends, and popular genres/artists to gain business insights.

📁 Project Overview
  This project includes SQL queries to answer common business questions using a relational music store dataset. It is ideal for showcasing your SQL proficiency, particularly in joins, aggregations, grouping, and    date/time functions.

🧠 Key Insights
    Here are the main insights the queries provide:

Top 5 Music Genres by Total Sales
  Identifies which music genres generate the most revenue based on track purchases.

Monthly Revenue Trend
  Analyzes revenue over time, grouped by month, to help understand sales seasonality and growth.

Top 5 Customers by Total Spend
  Reveals the most valuable customers in terms of lifetime purchase value.

Top 5 Artists by Track Sales
  Highlights the most successful artists based on total revenue from their tracks.
